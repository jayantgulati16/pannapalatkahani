## Jayant's original
Part 1 (60 s): 11:55 pm, Rohan tired from office boards the last metro, coach empty except an old woman in a white saree in the corner, old tiffin in hand, staring out into the dark. Next station: doors open, nobody boards. As the doors close Rohan notices she has NO reflection in the glass. He runs to the next coach, its door is locked. Turns: she is right behind him: "Beta, mera station aa gaya... par main utar nahi sakti." Black. Text: Aage kya hua? Part 2 ke liye comment karein!
Part 2 (60 s): She holds out the tiffin: "Daro mat beta, aaj mere bete ka birthday hai. Main uske liye uski pasand ki kheer layi thi." 5 years ago she died in a road accident outside this station. Her son still comes home on the last metro every night and goes to bed hungry, missing her. "Bas yeh tiffin mere bete ko de dena, woh agle station par utrega." Next station: a sad boy on the platform. Rohan turns, she is gone, only the warm tiffin on the seat. He gives it to the boy, who smells the kheer and cries: "Bilkul Maa ke haath jaisi khushboo hai."

## Review (Claude, 28 Nov)
1. Indian metro coaches have an open gangway, there is no locked door between coaches. Fix: he runs through the gangway, and in the next coach she is in the same corner seat with the same tiffin. Same again in the third coach. Then she is behind him.
2. Contradiction: if the son rides the last metro every night, why does she need Rohan, and why is he waiting on the platform? Fix: 5 years ago, on his birthday night, Maa was bringing kheer on the last metro while Bittu waited on the platform. Accident outside the station. Every birthday at 12:00 he still waits on that platform. 11:55 -> the clock hits 12:00 at the reveal.
3. "Main utar nahi sakti" needs one rule line: she never reached him; only someone alive can carry it out.
3b. A smell can't be shown on screen. Proof: the same steel tiffin with "बिट्टू" scratched on the lid by Maa + her chit "Happy birthday Bittu — Maa". Keep the khushboo line after that.
4. The reflection scare must read at a glance: Rohan's reflection is in the glass, her seat in the glass is EMPTY; push in.
5. Give Rohan his own arc: opening, "Maa calling" and he cuts the call ("baad mein"); ending, he calls Maa back: "Maa... khana khaya? Kal ghar aa raha hoon." This is the share/tag trigger.
6. Bittu alone on a bench with a small cake + one candle (instantly reads as a birthday) replaces "bhookha so jaata hai".
7. No real metro branding (no DMRC logo, no real line or station names): fictional "नगर मेट्रो", stations e.g. पुरानी चुंगी. Part 1 end = standing CLOSING VOICE rule + comment prompt.
8. Length ~65 s / ~70 s.

3D: almost all one set (a coach is repeated seats/poles/windows, reused for the 3-coach chase) + a platform set; real dolly shots and a true window reflection are what 3D adds. Cast: Rohan (from the Arjun 3D body: office shirt, bag, new hair), Maa (new: white saree, pallu over head), Bittu (young man).
