## The world (rules that must hold)
- Everyone is born with 10,00,000 words on a glowing wrist band. Every spoken word ticks it down. 0000 = voice gone forever.
- Laughing, crying and writing are free (Aarav "talks" with his sketchbook). Words can be TRANSFERRED by joining two bands.
- Court rule (board on the wall): "अदालत में दलील सिर्फ़ वकील की ज़ुबानी" → Meera can't just hand over the file; she has to SAY it.
- Rich people have lakhs of words (they buy them) — the rich lawyer talks and talks.
- Band colours: cyan = normal · red blinking = under 100 · red 0000 = mute · green = just received words.
- On screen, the meter counts EVERY spoken word live (big HUD number next to the speaker).

## Fixes vs the Gemini draft
1. Court rule planted → the 5-minute pressure makes sense.  2. Aarav sent 8,73,100 (not 9,98,800 — he had spoken as a child).
3. Meera always knew "someone" gave her words — she never knew who (planted in भाग 1 = comment bait).
4. The accident shattered her band → 0000.  5. Aarav kept 1,200 "to tell Meera one thing someday", not "for today".
6. Flashback = 15 years ago (Aarav 9, Meera 8) — little Aarav wears the SAME round glasses (rewatch clue).
