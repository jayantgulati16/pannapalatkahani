# पापा का मफ़लर — Papa's Muffler (Ep 2, 19 Nov 2026 · International Men's Day)

Style: Crayon Picture Book. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 48.5 s. No voice (Jayant's call: no AI voice, keeps monetisation safe):
music, foley and printed Devanagari words (Kalam). New characters: Papa (moustache, violet sweater, brown trousers) and
Tara (~7, two plaits with red ribbons, green school sweater, navy skirt, white socks). Papa's muffler: red with yellow stripes.
Tara's drawn muffler: teal with yellow stripes.

Trend: dense fog / cold wave in North India (IMD warnings, mid-Nov 2026) + International Men's Day (19 Nov).

## Logline
Every winter Papa wraps his own muffler round Tara and says he never feels cold — until she draws him one with her crayon,
and it becomes real. Then she draws a sun, and the fog melts.
