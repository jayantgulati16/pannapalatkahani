# बारिश वाली चिट्ठी — The Rain Letter

Style: Crayon Picture Book (`crayon-book`). Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~61 s. No voice: music-led, with the book's printed words in Devanagari (Kalam). (Offline Hindi TTS could not be speech-checked here, so the film runs on music, foley and printed words; a voice can be mixed in later.)
For YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Straight journey**: wide gali → boy shy → notes → rain → yes. Clear but the hook is slow (nothing happens in 3 s).
2. **In medias res mystery** (chosen): open on a blank note arriving on a clothesline and a puzzled girl. The question "why blank?" is the hook; the rain answers it.
3. **Two crayons argue**: his colour vs hers on one page, combining at the end. Pretty, but abstract for a Shorts audience.

Chosen: 2. A mystery in the first second, the answer at the peak, and the answer is the medium itself (wax resist).

## Logline and arc
A shy boy sends his neighbour blank notes across their shared clothesline, until the monsoon reveals what he wrote in white crayon.
- Setup: blank notes arrive; she is puzzled.
- Turn: rain wash crosses the page; three notes reveal three words: "चाय" "पर" "चलोगी?"
- Ending: she answers in bold red crayon, "हाँ!"; two cups of chai ride the line. The lane feels smaller (echo of the wide, distant opening).

## Benchmark
- Raymond Briggs-era hand-drawn picture-book animation: learn boiling texture, music-led tenderness, few words. Take no characters, song or images.
- Indian monsoon-romance cinema grammar: learn rain as the turn, chai as intimacy. Take no songs, shots or characters.

## Native moves
- **Wax resist** (signature): white crayon words invisible from frame one, revealed by the ultramarine wash.
- **Colouring is an action**: pink scribble = blushing; red crayon "हाँ" = her boldness vs his hidden white.
- **Drawn live**: the clothesline draws itself as a note travels.
- **It is a book**: pull out at the end to the page.

## Beat sheet and cue map
Tempo 96 bpm (beat 0.625 s), 6/8 lilt felt in 2 → bar = 1.875 s (3 beats of dotted feel).
- 0–9.6: music box motif (fragment), pulley squeaks on the action.
- 9.6–26.5: ukulele + flute, gentle, narration over.
- 26.5–35: woodblock ticks with each note arrival (accelerando by placement), clarinet low for the gag.
- 35–37.5: silence (room + distant thunder only).
- 37.5–46: glockenspiel note per revealed word; music box full theme enters at 40.
- 46–52.5: theme continues, ukulele strum on "हाँ", toy-piano flourish on the jump.
- 52.5–61: theme resolves, ritardando, last music-box note droops.
